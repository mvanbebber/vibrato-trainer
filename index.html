What is Vibrato Trainer?

Vibrato Trainer is a free, browser-based tempo-synced vibrato metronome for musicians, built on original research by Michael Van Bebber. His work reveals that vibrato is neither random nor a set rate per musician, but instead is a rhythmic subdivision of the musical pulse, and the specific subdivision changes predictably as tempo changes.

The discovery: vibrato as rhythmic subdivision

The insight — that vibrato rate is a function of tempo and subdivision, not a fixed speed — represents a new framework for understanding and teaching vibrato. Through analysis of performers across jazz, classical, pop, country, and R&B, Michael Van Bebber identified that vibrato oscillation rates consistently align with standard rhythmic subdivisions of the beat. This framework maps vibrato across six distinct tempo zones:

  • 50–63 BPM (Very Slow): Triplet sixteenth notes — 6 oscillations per beat
  • 63–100 BPM (Slow): Sixteenth notes — 4 oscillations per beat
  • 100–150 BPM (Medium): Triplet eighth notes — 3 oscillations per beat
  • 150–227 BPM (Medium-Fast): Eighth notes — 2 oscillations per beat
  • 227–300 BPM (Fast): Triplet quarter notes — 3 oscillations per 2 beats
  • 300–400 BPM (Very Fast): Quarter notes — 1 oscillation per beat

Van Bebber's research is the first to systematically map vibrato to the rhythmic grid across the full range of musical tempos. The zone boundaries above are posterior estimates from a Bayesian model fitted to 100 commercial recordings — see the research, and download the full dataset.

Who is it for?

Vibrato Trainer is designed for singers, string players (violin, viola, cello), guitar players, wind players (trumpet, saxophone, flute, clarinet), and jazz musicians of all kinds. Whether you're learning vibrato for the first time or refining your technique, this tool helps you lock your vibrato into the rhythmic grid.

Features

  • Tempo-synced vibrato click track
  • Tap tempo for any song
  • Visual dot animation showing vibrato rhythm in real time
  • Piano keyboard for pitch selection and vibrato tone playback
  • Adjustable time signatures (2/4, 3/4, 4/4 / 6/8, 9/8, 12/8)
  • Multiple waveforms: sawtooth, square, triangle, sine
  • Installable as a Progressive Web App — works offline
  • Free, no account required

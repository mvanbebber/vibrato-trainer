What is Vibrato Trainer?

Vibrato Trainer is a free, browser-based vibrato metronome for musicians. It automatically calculates the correct rhythmic subdivision for vibrato at any tempo — from slow ballads to fast bebop.

How vibrato rhythm works

Vibrato isn't random — it follows the pulse of the music. At different tempos, the oscillation rate that sounds most natural corresponds to different note values:

  • 50–75 BPM (Very Slow / Ballad): Triplet sixteenth notes — 6 oscillations per beat
  • 75–100 BPM (Slow / Soul): Sixteenth notes — 4 oscillations per beat
  • 100–150 BPM (Mid / Swing): Triplet eighth notes — 3 oscillations per beat
  • 150–200 BPM (Medium-Fast): Eighth notes — 2 oscillations per beat
  • 200–300 BPM (Fast / Bebop): Triplet quarter notes — 3 oscillations per 2 beats
  • 300–400 BPM (Double Time): Quarter notes — 1 oscillation per beat

Who is it for?

Vibrato Trainer is designed for singers, string players (violin, viola, cello, guitar), wind players (trumpet, saxophone, flute, clarinet), and jazz musicians of all kinds. Whether you're learning vibrato for the first time or refining your technique at advanced tempos, this tool helps you lock your vibrato into the rhythmic grid.

Features

  • Tempo-synced vibrato click track
  • Tap tempo for any song
  • Visual dot animation showing vibrato rhythm in real time
  • Piano keyboard for pitch selection and vibrato tone playback
  • Adjustable time signatures (2/4, 3/4, 4/4 / 6/8, 9/8, 12/8)
  • Multiple waveforms: sawtooth, square, triangle, sine
  • Installable as a Progressive Web App — works offline
  • Free, no account required

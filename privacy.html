Privacy Policy

Vibrato Trainer · vibratotrainer.com

Vibrato Trainer is a free tool for musicians. This policy explains what data is collected — and the short answer is: almost none.

Data We Collect

Vibrato Trainer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app runs entirely on your device. There are no user accounts, no login, and no data sent to any server.

No Analytics or Tracking

Vibrato Trainer does not use any analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking technologies. We do not use cookies or any equivalent technology to identify or follow users.

Audio

The app uses your device's audio system to play metronome clicks and tones. Audio is processed entirely on-device and is never recorded or transmitted.

Third-Party Services

Vibrato Trainer does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or data processors.

Children

Vibrato Trainer is appropriate for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL. The date below will reflect the most recent revision.
